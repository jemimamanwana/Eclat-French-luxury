The Couture Process

From sketch to final fitting - the journey of a couture creation

01

Consultation & Sketch

Each couture journey begins with a private consultation where our creative director translates your vision into initial sketches.

02

Fabric Selection

Clients are presented with the finest materials from our archives - rare silks, hand-woven fabrics, and exclusive embellishments.

03

Toile Creation

A prototype garment is crafted in muslin to perfect the cut and silhouette before cutting into the final fabric.

04

Hand Construction

Our master tailors and seamstresses handcraft each element using traditional techniques requiring hundreds of hours.

05

Embellishment

Our atelier of embroiderers, beaders, and feather workers add intricate details that define Eclat couture.

06

Final Fittings

Multiple fittings ensure the garment fits like a second skin, with adjustments made until perfection is achieved.
